About Quality Education (SDG 4)

Goal 4 of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals asks the world to "ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all."

In simple words, this means every child - no matter where they live, how much money their family has, or what background they come from - deserves the chance to go to school, learn important skills, and build a better future. Quality education is the foundation for reducing poverty, improving health, and creating equal opportunities for everyone.
